# Survival diary

## 14 October 1800
**The year the streets started to smell wrong**

This begins in 1800, though the newspapers of the time called it something else. A new infection appeared and the governments did what they always do: they covered it with a small word. "Common flu", they said. We all believed it once, as children, in the kitchen at home, with a hot forehead and a cup of broth.

I believed it too. I write the date because somebody has to write it: the day it began, no one else will remember.

---
## 22 October
**The Great Lie: what on earth the "Green Flu" is**

The dead here are not dead. I write it slowly, because it is the only thing worth understanding: the pathogen does not raise corpses. It is a mutant virus, a strain of hyper-accelerated rabies crossed with influenza.

CEDA - the government's emergency agency - found out. And to avoid panic across the United States it gave it that ridiculous name: "Green Flu".

The infected feel no pain. Their adrenaline climbs to levels that are not human: they run like Olympic athletes, they shove like oxen. We call them Ghouls. Bluish skin, blue eyes glowing in the dark as if a candle burned behind them.

~~ That they can be reasoned with, that talking is enough. A lie from the consulates.

## 3 November
**What they say in the ports**

When the discovery went around the world, every alarm went off. The countries that still lack the technology will fare worse than anyone. Napoleon Bonaparte and the other kings are in fatal trouble: an ordered army is useless against something that does not respect ranks.

And the virus does not sit still. It mutates with the habits of each country, with genetics and with the health of each person. Every region breeds different offspring.

- Margin note: they say there is an intelligence system that records everything and lets you plan a million ideas. If you see someone attempting an action in chat and you feel like testing whether you can do the same: don't. Everything has its requirements, and there are many.

## 9 November
**Rules of the trade: falling is not dying**

When your hearts reach zero you do not die: you fall. You lie on the ground, incapacitated, looking at boots and cobblestones.

Another member of the team has to come close and hold the interaction for five whole seconds, without taking a single hit, to lift you up.

If the incapacitation bar reaches zero, that is it: real death, spectator mode, no inventory loss. The body stays where it fell, as a reminder for whoever comes next. To bring the dead back you need a defibrillator, and those only come out of loot.

---
## 15 November
**The PINs**

Some attacks from special mutations strip away all control. They grab you and that is that: you can do nothing, and no brilliant idea will get you out. Every attempt is closed, even the clever ones.

Only your team can free you. That is why exploring alone is a guaranteed death sentence. And when someone is caught, a global warning sounds that everyone hears: that bell means a companion is hanging from a pin.

---
## 21 November
**Where these things come from (I): the Ghoul**

The most basic of the millions of mutations: the default being consumed by the Compound V virus. An ordinary zombie, but with blue skin and blue eyes.

Compound V in its blood grants resistance, strength, speed and faster hits. A single one lands three hits in one and a half seconds. Its only passive ability is reach: three blocks of distance.

And they pile up. When someone starts stacking blocks to escape, they climb on one another until they reach him.

- Difficulty: 1 skull.

---
## 27 November
**Where these things come from (II): the Jockey**

One of the first specials with a grab system. It is small, it looks like a dreadful monkey, and it attacks by latching on from behind or from the front: it baits you, waits, and takes you by surprise.

It shifts its weight to make you stagger wherever it wants. It has control over the victim and only lets go if it is killed.

It can drag you toward natural hazards, toward Witches, toward other infected, or straight to the edge of a cliff so you hang there, or kill you if you do not resist.

- Origin: it arrived with the stories of Left 4 Dead 2. An infected that jumps on your back and will not let go is the same nightmare told with another accent.
- Difficulty: 2 skulls.

---
## 4 December
**Where these things come from (III): the Cuirassier**

This one is not a rag. It is a short-range enemy with massive health, armed with a heavy sabre and dressed in a Napoleonic metal cuirass.

Its most dangerous ability is the charge: it roars, accelerates to extreme speed and rams in a straight line or a curve, dealing massive impalement damage. Whoever it runs through is left in the Stabbed state: on the ground, bleeding for a long while. Neither a defibrillator nor a medkit cures that.

It blocks every kind of projectile and sends it back at the shooter with the damage multiplied by 1.25. It can even block melee attacks if it predicts you: that is why closing in with a blade is now the only real way to hurt it.

- Origin: it comes from Guts & Blackpowder. The cuirass belongs to the heavy cavalry of the Napoleonic era; here it is worn by an infected that no longer obeys any emperor, which is why it is more frightening.
- Field note: against one or two survivors it eats your time and keeps you busy. Against four or more it becomes a vulnerable lobster, but it will choose when to use the charge.
- Difficulty: 5 skulls.

## 11 December
**Races: what we carry inside**

On first joining you may be born human, Shark, Cyborg, or one more of the +300 races in the catalogue. They support the player: extra help to fight against high difficulties.

They evolve: V1, V2, V3, V4 and beyond. Each stage demands actions or missions according to the rarity of the race.

- Do you have a race? It is checked with Check race rarity, between asterisks, at the start and at the end. A notification arrives with the rarity you were given.

Rarities, from the most common to the hardest to obtain, also catalogue artefacts and items:

- Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra Rare, Legendary
- Obsainan, Mythic, Godly, Eternal, Fabled

---
## 19 December
**Difficulties, restarts and the light above your head**

Every difficulty change brings new challenges and global events. The most basic one is that mobs raise their stats.

- Easy, Normal, Advanced, Expert
- INSURGENCY I-V, INFERNUM I-V
- BACKBREAKING I-X (in progress)
- DOOMSDAY I-X (in progress)
- ARMAGEDDON I-X (in progress)

Every player carries a white icon above their head: it is there to find each other again. It reaches more than 3,000 blocks of distance and shows even if those chunks are not loaded yet.

The server is still in constant development, with daily updates. That is why restarts never fail to appear: they are daily, but they are few.

---
## 28 December
**Last page for now**

Abandoned in some shelter, on an island, in a half-fallen building. Adapt your weapons, perfect your tactics and survive.

You will have enough resources to survive safe and sound. Or perhaps you will not, and in time they will run out.

I write so as not to forget. If anyone finds this notebook: the year was 1800, the lie was called Green Flu, and those who ran were not dead.
