## Version 0.5.7

**A clock stopped at eleven fifty-two.**

A pocket watch has appeared in the upper right corner of the diary. Its bronze hands always mark the same hour — the instant when everything stopped.

Below the watch, carved in small letters, the chronicle you carry with you.

### The city breathes more easily
- The diary now adapts more gracefully to any window size.
- The official notices and the wanted poster find their place without crowding.
- On smaller screens, the pages simplify on their own, showing only what matters.

### A detail in the margin
At the foot of the last page, if you look closely, you will find a signature written in sepia ink. Anno Domini MDCCCXCVII.

## Version 0.4.4

**The city has come to life.**

The streets no longer remain silent. When you gaze at the main menu you will hear the pulse of a metropolis trapped by the Green Flu.

### Voices of the city
- The wind runs through the cobbled alleys.
- Rain patters upon the stones.
- A distant murmur rises from the rooftops.

### Shadows that move
- Hurried steps fade into the fog.
- Old timbers creak beneath an unseen weight.
- A door swings open somewhere far away.

### Omens
- The church bells ring for no apparent reason.
- A thunderclap breaks the smoke-laden sky.
- Crows announce what no one wishes to see.
- The echo of chains rises from the cellars.

### Voices in many tongues
The quarantine knows no borders. The diary now speaks in twenty-five tongues, so that no soul is left without understanding the warning.

## Version 0.2.5

**The diary looks more alive than ever.**

The pages have been restored with care. Every detail seeks to convey the atmosphere of a city in darkness.

- Candles glow with a warm and trembling halo.
- Bloodstains show themselves starkly upon the paper.
- Wax seals rise with relief and shadow.
- Floral ornaments frame the most important sections.
- Aged paper covers the pages without cuts or borders.

### New strokes
- Smeared ink stains.
- Quill strokes beneath the titles.
- Torn edges on some sheets.
- A special mode for the darkest scenes.
