# Eternal Craft: Green Flu
## Update Notes

## Version 0.8.3

**Some silences are kept out of respect.**

When the parish bells toll in the distance, when thunder splits the sky, or when crows take flight among the rooftops, the music of the city steps aside with discretion. It does not fall silent; it only lowers its voice so that the omens may be heard clearly. Once the moment passes, the melody returns to the fore without haste.

The threads of memory have also grown firmer. Even if one looks away from the diary for a moment or seeks other notes, the song of the city is no longer interrupted without cause. It persists, accompanying the observer in his vigil.

---

## Version 0.8.2

**The melodies weave together without a break.**

Before, when a piece ended, silence fell abruptly. It is different now. Before one melody fades completely, the next already begins to whisper underneath. Both share the room for a few seconds like two voices in a single chamber, until one bids farewell and the other arrives. The seam is invisible.

---

## Version 0.8.1

**Now you may decide how much you wish to hear.**

Not all ears are alike. A small control has been arranged so that the reader may decide the strength with which the city's lament reaches him. He may silence it if the moment calls for calm, or let it fly free if his spirit allows. The adjustment is immediate and the melody settles at once, obeying the hand.

---

## Version 0.8.0

**The city has found its song.**

For a long time, the street spoke only with its own noises: the wind, the rain, and the echo of those who walk. But now, from somewhere we cannot point to, music has arrived. Four melodies take turns in the night, mingling with the storm without covering it. It is the first time this diary has a voice of its own to accompany it.
